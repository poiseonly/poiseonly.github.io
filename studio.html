THE STUDIO

spaces that stay with you

29thAvenueDesigns is a full-spectrum interior design studio based in Lagos, Nigeria, rooted in the belief that a well-designed space doesn't just look good, it feels like something. It feels like you.

We design with intention. Every material chosen, every corner considered, every detail deliberate. Because the spaces you live and work in shape how you feel every single day, and that is not something we take lightly.

At 29thAvenueDesigns, we go beyond aesthetics. We take time to understand who you are, how you move through a space, and what you need to truly feel at home in it. We listen first. Then we create. And what we create is an experience that is entirely, specifically yours. No two spaces are the same. Neither are the people in them.

Adedamola Samuel

Founder & Principal Designer

Adedamola's path into design started with an unusual foundation — Materials Engineering. Long before he was designing rooms, he was studying how materials fail, endure, and perform. That technical grounding became the lens through which he now sees space: not just what looks beautiful, but what will hold up, what will feel right to the touch years later, what a material's texture does to how a person feels standing in a room.

That's the belief at the center of 29th Avenue — that a well considered material choice is what makes a space linger in someone's memory, long after they've left it.

He believes that intentional design is one of the most powerful ways we can honour the spaces we live in and that belief sits at the heart of everything 29thAvenueDesigns creates.
