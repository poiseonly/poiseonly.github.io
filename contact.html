OUR PROCESS

From consultation to creation

STEP 01
Your First Move

However you reach us, WhatsApp, a call, or the form on this page. You'll hear from us fast. From the first message, you are treated like a project already in motion.

STEP 02
Discovery Call

We schedule a call at your convenience to understand your vision, requirements, and lifestyle needs.

STEP 03
Site Assessment

Our team schedule a planned visit to your space to evaluate its layout, structure and potential. We want to ensure every design decision is tailored to your design style and taste.

STEP 04
Design Development

This is where the story gets written, spatial layouts, materials selection, bespoke pieces, 3D renders. Every choice, down to the smallest details is made to reflect who you are. Not a template.

STEP 05
Execution and Oversight

We run the site so you don't have to. Craftsmen, suppliers, timelines are coordinated end to end, with progress updates that keep you informed without needing to be there.

STEP 06
The Reveal

A space that finally feels like yours, considered, crafted, ready to live in.
